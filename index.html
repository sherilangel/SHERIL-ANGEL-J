    The abstract

    I am a research-driven PhD scholar in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a Teaching Research Associate at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore. My work spans Natural Language Processing, text classification, social-media analysis, AI-based mental-health screening and computer vision, and I take research problems all the way from definition to model development, evaluation and publication.

    I bring structured, critical thinking and academic rigour with a practical, solution-oriented mindset, and I enjoy turning doctoral research into real-world innovation alongside experts from technical and business domains.
